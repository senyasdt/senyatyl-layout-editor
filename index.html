Keyboard Layout Editor

Drag keys. Arrow keys move the selected key by 1 unit; hold Shift for 10. Q/E rotate by 1 degree; Shift+Q/E rotate by 5 degrees. Rotation is exported as ZMK tenths of a degree.
Drop a Keymap Editor JSON, `.dtsi`, or pasted DTSI keys block here. The editor detects the format and redraws immediately.
